# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
High-end event planners, elite couples planning luxury weddings, and diplomats/corporates.

## Product Purpose
To showcase "The Embassy Catering" (Delhi NCR's luxury multi-cuisine caterer since 1948) through a cinematic, seven-moment storytelling digital experience that evokes trust, prestige, legacy, and culinary excellence.

## Brand Personality
Luxurious, Historic, and Masterful. Employs a cinematic, narrative-driven flow that establishes high emotional prestige.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS/corporate templates, neon colors, gradient text, tech-focused dark modes, side-stripe borders, and generic card grids.

## Design Principles
- **Orchestrated Storytelling**: Lead with a narrative-driven structure (the Seven-Moment Film) to guide users through an emotional journey rather than just listing services.
- **Cinematic Whitespace & Spacing**: Spacing is prestige. High-end luxury relies on generous, rhythmic margins and breathable layouts.
- **Masterful Contrast & Saturation**: Deep color saturation (e.g., Embassy Red) paired with high-contrast text ratios (>= 4.5:1) for readability without sacrificing luxury character.
- **Intentional, Non-Obtrusive Motion**: Use smooth, organic animations (Lenis scrolling, Framer Motion) that enhance visual interest while respecting user motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Strict compliance with WCAG AA standards (high-contrast text ratios >= 4.5:1) and prefers-reduced-motion media query support to bypass complex animations.
